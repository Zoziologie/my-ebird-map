Boundary sources

These are approximate map illustrations, adapted by merging, rekeying and simplifying boundaries. Observation membership and counts follow the eBird export. All polygons are simplified at 1,000 m while preserving source island parts.

Synthetic illustrations

Denmark, Libya and Qatar use nearest-centre partitions clipped to country land, with centres derived from eBird region bounds. These are not administrative boundaries. Jaghbub and Al Qatrun seed coordinates credit GeoNames under CC BY 4.0. Perseverance and Kingman Reef show eBird bounding boxes, not land or reef outlines. The app marks synthetic areas with dashed borders and a popup explanation.

Natural Earth

Most boundaries use Natural Earth public-domain admin-1 and country data. Puerto Rico uses its admin-2 municipios.
