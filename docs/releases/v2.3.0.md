# My eBird Map 2.3.0

Prepared on 28 October 2026. This minor release adds checklist replay in beta and improves map colors and geographic navigation, preserving the existing import format and browser storage.

## Changes

- Replay cumulative species, checklists, observations, numeric bird counts, or locations through time using the top metric buttons, an activity timeline with year ticks, play/pause, seeking, and speed controls.
- Replay individual locations, countries, or regions, with cumulative colors, visit pulses, and animated polygon counters. Locations and areas remain invisible until their first checklist. Replay includes all matching locations regardless of the current viewport and starts at their full extent.
- Switch replay views while retaining progress, metric, speed, and playback state. Appearance settings preserve progress; date and additional-taxa filters restart with the new selection. Pan, zoom, and selected-species clearing remain available. Imports and polygon drawing stay unavailable during replay.
- Use shared continuous logarithmic purple–teal–yellow scales in every normal and replay map view, with exact maximum labels and stable full-file scales. Selected taxa receive their own scales.
- Fit country and region clicks to complete polygon boundaries and anchor popups at their geographic center. Illustrative boundaries use normal styling while retaining their explanatory popup notes.
- Reduce repeated counter layout work, cache boundary bounds, and remove obsolete discrete color calculations. Replay uses compact daily summaries, static geometry, feature-state updates, and at most 150 visible area counters; temporary layers, listeners, and animation frames are cleaned up on exit.

## Validation

App checks, 45 unit tests, lint, formatting, production build, and bundle budgets pass. All 12 desktop/mobile Chromium and WebKit browser checks pass, including live Mapbox integration. Additional browser checks verified polygon fitting and popup anchoring, unseen-area opacity, replay view changes, preserved progress across projection/background changes, filter restarts, and switching back to individual locations without Mapbox errors. The main JavaScript bundle is 66.9 KiB gzip against an 80 KiB budget.

## Compatibility and limitations

Replay is a beta feature. Imported observations currently retain dates rather than checklist start times, so all checklists on a date advance together. Long gaps are compressed. Area counters briefly interpolate for display and settle to exact values when paused, scrubbed, or complete. Location clusters and custom-area drawing do not offer replay. Existing taxonomy, regional exotic-status, illustrative-boundary, and real-account Google Drive limitations remain unchanged. Automated device emulation does not establish physical-phone performance.
